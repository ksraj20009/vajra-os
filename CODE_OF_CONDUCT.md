# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Vajra OS is built in India for everyone. We pledge to keep this project
and its community open, respectful and welcoming to all — regardless of
age, caste, creed, gender, language, nationality, religion, sexual
orientation, or any other part of who someone is.

India speaks many languages and follows many traditions; so does this
project. Disagree about code as much as you like — treat people with
respect always.

## Our standards

**Examples of behaviour that keeps the community healthy:**

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members

**Examples of unacceptable behaviour:**

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political
  attacks
- Harassment of any kind, in any of India's languages or in English
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Other conduct which a reasonable person would consider inappropriate
  in a professional setting

## Our responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards of acceptable
behaviour and are expected to take appropriate and fair corrective
action in response to any instance of unacceptable behaviour.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may
be reported by contacting the maintainer via a GitHub issue marked
**[conduct]** (or, if privacy is needed, by requesting a private channel
first). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and
fairly.

All community members — maintainers included — are held to this
standard. A maintainer who violates it is held to the same consequences.
